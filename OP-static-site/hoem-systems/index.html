HOEM Smart Energy Systems

Bring your home, EV charger and solar together under one smart energy platform. HOEM lets you see where your power is going and automatically shift loads to cheaper, cleaner energy.

HOEM-ready switchboards

Clean, labelled boards wired so HOEM can see and manage key loads like hot water, pool pumps and EV charging.

Bill-friendly automation

Shift heavy loads to off-peak and solar hours automatically – no more guessing timers or watching the meter.

Future-proof installs

Designed so you can add EV chargers, batteries or more solar down the track without re-doing the whole board.

See HOEM in action

HOEM is an Australian smart energy platform that sits over the top of your existing gear – solar, EV charger, hot water, air-con and more – and helps you use power when it’s cheapest and cleanest.

  • Live view of what’s using power in your home.
  • Rules to charge EVs or heat water when solar is exporting.
  • Protection against overloading the house supply.
  • Works beautifully for RV homebase, sheds and man-caves too.
